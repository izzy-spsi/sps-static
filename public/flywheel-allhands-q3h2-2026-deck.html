Q3 · Jul–Sep 2026

This quarter, it goes live.

Rider App

The new Rider App is live across the US and Mexico, replacing the old app. We measure every social channel — organic and paid — across the full funnel to conversion, and run structured promotion campaigns to acquire new riders and re-engage existing ones.

Driver Payouts

A double-entry accounting ledger records every trip transaction — fare, tips, cancellation fees, incentives — across card and cash, reconciling the full path from rider payment to driver payout, including negative balances.

Uber 3P Demand

We close the feature gap with Uber: editing multiple checkpoints mid-route, mid-trip cancellations, Uber's driver incentives (DxGy and PTB) paid automatically through the API, and Uber Reserve pre-booked trips.

Flywheel Central

An all-in-one portal for government agencies to run their subsidised-ride programs — managing patrons, trips, and reimbursements in one place. Folds in the existing Late Shift Connect program.

Fleet Portal

A complete operating product for fleet owners: real-time live operations, vehicle and driver asset management, reporting and analytics, and corporate-account administration.

Support Portal

One internal portal where Support and Ops can see live activity across every market and resolve any rider, fleet, or driver request — backed by full reporting.

Sequencing

Where we're being careful.

The roadmap is honest about its own pressure points. Here's where capacity is tight, and how we're handling it.

Rider App → Mexico handoff5-DAY OVERLAP
US launch close-out (through Aug 14) overlaps the start of Mexico operating-mode work (Aug 10) by five days.
Mitigation: dedicated mobile capacity split, or treat Aug 10–14 as discovery-only.
Legacy Rider App sunsetSEQUENCING
Sunset work (Aug 3–31) starts before the US launch target even closes (Aug 14).
Mitigation: mandatory-update work must not assume production approval before Aug 14.
Ledger v1 → v2THIN BUFFER
v1 (6 weeks, Aug 3–Sep 15) is compressed while Finance/identity policy gates remain open; v2 (multi-party settlement) starts just two weeks after v1 ends.
Mitigation: resolve Finance/identity gates early; treat the 2-week gap as stabilization, not slack.
v2 + Mexico distributionRESOURCE CLASH
Mexico payment distribution (Oct 15–Nov 30) runs concurrently with v2 on the same Backoffice/payment architecture.
Mitigation: a distinct team, or explicit shared sequencing — not two hard problems on one team at once.
Uber ReserveNO BUFFER
Starts Aug 15 — one day after Incentives and Mid-Trip Cancellations targets close (Aug 14). Cancellations' end date is Uber-controlled (their admin testing), not fully ours to commit to.
Mitigation: retain contingency beyond Aug 14 in any external commitment.
Support Portal · ActionsAGGRESSIVE
One month (November) for refund, payout, and OTA actions — a meaningfully harder phase than the read-only Intelligence layer.
Mitigation: use October as explicit API/permissions hardening, not just buffer.

Metrics

What we're still defining.

A small analytics workstream running alongside the build — not an afterthought.

Acquisition-funnel conversion

Social impression/click → install → first ride, organic and paid.

Needs: funnel stages + conversion definition. Tracking stack (SDK, Firebase, UTM) already exists.

Payout accuracy

Correctness/completeness of the ledger's driver payouts.

Needs: discrepancy/reconciliation rate + tolerance. Only measurable once the ledger is live.

New demand accounts

Active government agencies / corporate accounts.

Needs: "active account" definition + source of record (Central portal).

Support resolution

Speed and quality of Support & Ops request handling.

Needs: time-to-resolution / resolution-rate definition + ticketing source.

We don't put a number on a slide until it's real. Defining these is part of the plan, not an afterthought.
