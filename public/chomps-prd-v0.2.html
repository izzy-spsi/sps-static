01 — Overview

What is Chomps?

Chomps is Domo Cafe's fan membership platform — not a points-redemption program. There is no spendable balance and nothing to "cash in." Chomps is a lifetime accumulation score, named after Domo-kun's iconic open-mouth "chomp," that never decreases. Crossing a threshold permanently unlocks a new membership tier with standing privileges — closer to airline elite status or a game rank than a punch card or rewards-points wallet.


Unlike traditional restaurant loyalty programs, Chomps works globally. A fan in Tokyo who can't visit Buena Park can still earn Chomps by following on Instagram, buying merch online, or completing digital challenges. When the online store launches, Chomps is the currency that connects the entire ecosystem.

03 — Unlock Model

Milestone Unlocks + Standing Tier Perks — Nothing to Redeem

Chomps is never spent and never decremented. There is no redemption menu and no "pay N Chomps for X" transaction of any kind. Instead, Chomps works two ways: milestone unlocks (a one-time unlock the moment a member first crosses a threshold) and standing tier perks (an ongoing privilege a member holds for as long as they remain in that tier). Both are permanent unlocks, not purchases against a balance — the Chomps number itself never goes down, no matter how many perks a member uses.

Active for as long as the member holds that tier (see Section 04 for annual re-qualification). Using a perk never reduces Chomps — it's a benefit of status, not a purchase.
No redemption menu, period. There is no equivalent of "Full Entrée = 8,000 Chomps" or "Any Drink = 4,000 Chomps." Food/drink perks (free item, BOGO drink) only ever exist as a standing perk of a tier — never something purchased with a Chomps balance. This intentionally removes any "Domo-branded item sold for points" mechanic from the model.
04 — Membership Tiers

The Domo Fan Journey

Four tiers, unlocked by Chomps earned. Higher tiers unlock multipliers, early access, reservations, and exclusive experiences.
Two different numbers, on purpose (decided 2026-07-14):

1. Lifetime Chomps (never resets, ever). The permanent, all-time accumulation score. This is a member's history with the brand — it only ever goes up, drives "member since" recognition, and gates the one-time milestone unlocks in Section 03 (first pin at 10,000, etc.).

2. Active Tier (rolling 12-month re-qualification, like airline status). A member's current, active tier is based on Chomps earned in the trailing 12 months, reviewed on a fixed cadence (e.g., annually), not continuously. Staying active keeps or advances tier. Going quiet for a year can soft-drop a member to the tier their trailing-12mo activity actually supports — but their lifetime Chomps total is untouched. This is a scheduled review-point change, not month-to-month volatility, and should include a grace/warning period before any downgrade.

Why: a pure lifetime-permanent top tier only ever grows and never has scarcity — within a couple years it stops meaning anything and becomes operationally unmanageable (e.g., "priority table always" and "direct line to Izzy" can't scale to an ever-growing, mostly-inactive list). Rolling re-qualification keeps every tier — especially Legend — meaningful, without needing a full annual reset of the whole program (which would feel punishing and kill the motivating "status forever" hook).

05 — Waitlist & Reservations

From Walk-In to Valued Guest

Currently walk-in only with no waitlist. A digital waitlist solves three problems at once: guest experience, Chomps enrollment, and operational data.
The enrollment moment: A guest waiting for a table has their phone in hand, nothing to do, and high motivation. This is the highest-converting Chomps enrollment window you'll ever have. Guest scans door QR → joins waitlist with phone number → immediately offered Chomps signup → 500 Chomps on today's visit if they sign up now.
🔢 Digital Waitlist (Phase 2)
Guest scans QR at entrance → enters phone → joins waitlist → gets SMS when table is ready. Frees guests to browse merch, explore, or sit outside instead of standing in line. Captures every phone number for Chomps enrollment.

Legend Tier. Achieving the Chomps threshold for Domo Legend status does not guarantee Legend membership. Legend invitations are curated and issued solely at the discretion of GFC BP LLC on an annual review cadence.

07 — Automated Tracking & Anti-Fraud

Every Earn Channel Must Be Automatic and Hard to Game

Every earn channel is tracked automatically against a verifiable event — no manual/self-reported entry as the default path. This matters more here than in a typical points program: because Chomps only unlocks status and never decrements, a bad actor who games their way to a high tier holds something permanent and hard to revoke without an ugly public rollback. Fraud prevention has to be tighter than a normal spend-down points program, not looser.
🔐 One Real Identity Per Member
Phone number is the unique account anchor (matches Toast's SMS-based order-matching model). Duplicate-account and multi-accounting checks (device/SIM-level signals) run at signup and on suspicious earn patterns, not just at redemption time — there is no redemption step to catch abuse at, so it has to be caught at the point of earning.
✅ Verified-Event Earning Only
  • In-cafe purchase — Toast order tied to phone. Highest trust, hardest to fake.
  • Social follow — OAuth-verified, one-time, not self-reported.
  • Social tag/story/review — webhook-confirmed where possible; anything relying on manual verification (e.g. Yelp/Google review) is capped low-value and rate-limited by design.
  • Referral — phone/device fingerprint checked to block self-referral and burner-account farming.
  • WhoRiddle partnership — inherits WhoRiddle's own anti-bot integrity; treated as a lower-trust channel and capped accordingly until proven otherwise.
System-enforced caps, not policy-only limits. High-risk / easily-gamed channels (story tags, reviews, self-reported actions) have hard caps the system will not award past, regardless of activity volume — this is enforced in code, not just written into the Terms of Service.
Fraud-review gate before top-tier crossing. Automatic promotion applies for Domo Friend/Fan/Obsessed. Crossing into consideration for Domo Legend triggers a review step (see Section 04) rather than instant automatic promotion, given the effectively-permanent nature of top-tier status.

11 — Data Model

Core Data Schema

PostgreSQL via Prisma. Every table feeds both the loyalty system and the forecasting engine.
-- Core member identity members { id UUID PRIMARY KEY phone VARCHAR UNIQUE -- primary in-cafe identity email VARCHAR UNIQUE -- for remote fans name VARCHAR birthdate DATE chomps_balance INTEGER DEFAULT 0 -- current spendable balance chomps_lifetime INTEGER DEFAULT 0 -- never decrements — used for tier tier ENUM(friend, fan, obsessed, legend) referral_code VARCHAR UNIQUE referred_by UUID REFERENCES members created_at TIMESTAMPTZ last_activity TIMESTAMPTZ } -- Every Chomps transaction (earn or spend) transactions { id UUID PRIMARY KEY member_id UUID REFERENCES members type ENUM(earn, redeem, expire, adjust) source ENUM(purchase_fb, purchase_merch, social_follow, social_tag, social_tiktok, referral, signup, survey, birthday, online_store, manual, milestone) chomps INTEGER -- positive=earn, negative=spend reference_id VARCHAR -- Toast order ID, post URL, etc. metadata JSONB created_at TIMESTAMPTZ } -- In-cafe visits — links Toast order to Chomps member visits { id UUID PRIMARY KEY member_id UUID REFERENCES members toast_order_id VARCHAR date DATE daypart ENUM(lunch, dinner) fb_revenue DECIMAL(10,2) merch_revenue DECIMAL(10,2) covers INTEGER chomps_earned INTEGER multiplier DECIMAL(4,2) DEFAULT 1.0 } -- Social platform connections social_connections { id UUID PRIMARY KEY member_id UUID REFERENCES members platform ENUM(instagram, tiktok, yelp, google) handle VARCHAR platform_id VARCHAR -- platform's user ID for webhook matching access_token TEXT ENCRYPTED follows_domo BOOLEAN verified_at TIMESTAMPTZ } -- Digital waitlist waitlist { id UUID PRIMARY KEY phone VARCHAR member_id UUID REFERENCES members NULLABLE party_size INTEGER position INTEGER status ENUM(waiting, seated, left, no_show) joined_at TIMESTAMPTZ seated_at TIMESTAMPTZ wait_minutes INTEGER -- actual wait time — feeds forecast }
Forecasting bridge: The visits table links every Chomps member to real revenue, daypart, and covers. The waitlist table captures actual wait times and party sizes, feeding projected cover accuracy. Together they transform anonymous Toast data into attributed guest intelligence.
