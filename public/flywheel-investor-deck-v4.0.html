The Revenue Engine
Revenue and EBITDA growth from FYE2025 actuals through 2029F - driven by three product engines (Flywheel Advantage, Connect, D2D), real named fleet contracts, and the D2D Nationwide rollout. Conservative scenario.
Net Revenue ($M) CAGR --
  • FYE2025 actual ($31.4M) is the real, audited starting point - not a projection.
  • Growth driven by three confirmed product engines: Flywheel Advantage (hardware-installed), Connect (3rd-party fleet dispatch), and D2D (driver app) - not one single lever.
  • D2D Nationwide rollout (40,000 vehicles, 8 tranches of 5,000 every 90 days from Apr 2027) is the single largest contributor from 2027 onward.
EBITDA ($M) CAGR -- ○ Margin %
  • EBITDA margin expands from 13.3% (FYE2025) to 21.4% (2029F) as fixed Payroll/Opex costs are spread across a fast-growing revenue base.
  • Hardware CapEx (Flywheel Advantage only - Connect/D2D require no in-vehicle equipment) is capitalized and depreciated over 24 months, not expensed against EBITDA in the month of purchase.
  • Payroll scales at roughly half the rate of fleet growth - real operating leverage, not a flat-cost assumption.

KISS Conversion Mechanics - Identical Terms Across All 9 Investors
Path A - Next Equity Financing (Section 2.1): Triggered by any Preferred Stock sale of ≥$3M gross proceeds - the Uber $26.5M round qualifies. KISS converts into the same Preferred Stock class Uber receives, at the greater of (i) 20% discount to Uber's price or (ii) $25M valuation cap ÷ fully-diluted cap. This is the expected path given the Uber round in progress.
Path B - Maturity Conversion (Section 2.3): If 24 months pass from a note's issuance with no qualifying financing, the Majority in Interest may force conversion into a new series of Series Seed Preferred Stock (per seriesseed.com standard terms). Several 2022-vintage notes have already passed their 24-month maturity date.
Key terms (identical across all 9 KISS notes, amounts vary only): 10% annual interest (compounded), $25M valuation cap, 20% discount, 24-month maturity, general unsecured obligation. Participation rights: Major Investors (≥$100K aggregate) may invest up to 1x their KISS principal in the Next Equity Financing on the same terms - up to $3.62M of the Uber round's allocation could be exercised by existing holders rather than new money. Hall Group (Hall Trust, Bluejacket, Makel Family Partnership, Dietz Family Trust, Dietz Revocable Trust, Greiner) holds a standing board observer seat, currently Michael J. Hall. Not converting to common stock under either path.

Appendix: How to Read These Numbers
Definitions and reconciliation notes - added 2026-07-22 following a full financial-basis review. Use this page to answer any question about which revenue figure is being shown and why.
Definitions
TermDefinition
GMV / Total RevenueGross fare volume across all channels (Ehail/Deliveries + Uber Ride Hails + Uber Referral + Service/Fee Income), including driver pass-through. NOT what Flywheel retains.
Net Revenue (= Gross Profit)GMV minus Cost of Services. This is what Flywheel actually earns and keeps - the correct basis for growth/margin narratives.
EBITDAEarnings before interest, tax, depreciation & amortization, computed directly from the model/P&L. This is plain EBITDA, not "Adjusted EBITDA" - no non-GAAP adjustments have been applied or documented.
