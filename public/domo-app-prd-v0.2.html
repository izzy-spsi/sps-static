01
Loyalty Program

Chomps — Fan Membership, Not a Points Wallet

Chomps is Domo Cafe's fan membership platform — not a points-redemption program. There is no spendable balance and nothing to "cash in." Chomps is a lifetime accumulation score, named after Domo-kun's iconic open-mouth "chomp," that never decreases. Crossing a threshold permanently unlocks a new membership tier with standing privileges — closer to airline elite status or a game rank than a punch card or rewards-points wallet.

Unlike traditional restaurant loyalty programs, Chomps works globally. A fan in Tokyo who can't visit Buena Park can still earn Chomps by following on Instagram, buying merch online, or completing digital challenges. When the online store launches, Chomps is the currency that connects the entire ecosystem.

🍽️ In-Cafe 📱 Social 🌐 Online
Strategic Purpose Beyond Loyalty

Chomps is the identity and data layer for the entire Domo Cafe intelligence system. Every member ties a real person to every purchase, social action, and visit — powering the forecasting model, measuring marketing impact, tracking return rates, and feeding the scenario engine.

How Fans Earn Chomps

10 Chomps per $1 spent. Every interaction earns. $1.99 = 19 Chomps (floor to nearest 10 cents). Bigger numbers create stronger emotional engagement — "You earned 347 Chomps tonight!" lands differently than "You earned 34."

Dynamic Multipliers

Off-peak hours (weekday lunch, slow periods) automatically trigger bonus earn rates to drive traffic. Example: Monday 11am–2pm → 2x Chomps on all F&B. Triggered by Toast occupancy data — tables below 40% → multiplier activates.

Milestone Unlocks + Standing Tier Perks — Nothing to Redeem

Chomps is never spent and never decremented. There is no redemption menu and no "pay N Chomps for X" transaction of any kind. Instead, Chomps works two ways: milestone unlocks (a one-time unlock the moment a member first crosses a threshold) and standing tier perks (an ongoing privilege a member holds for as long as they remain in that tier). Both are permanent unlocks, not purchases against a balance — the Chomps number itself never goes down, no matter how many perks a member uses.

The Domo Fan Journey — Four Tiers

Two Different Numbers, On Purpose (decided 2026-07-14)

1. Lifetime Chomps (never resets, ever) — the permanent all-time score, gates the one-time milestone unlocks above.
2. Active Tier (rolling 12-month re-qualification, like airline status) — a member's current tier is based on Chomps earned in the trailing 12 months, reviewed on a fixed cadence. Going quiet for a year can soft-drop a member's active tier without touching their lifetime total. Scheduled review-point change, not month-to-month volatility — includes a grace/warning period before any downgrade.

Why: a pure lifetime-permanent top tier only ever grows and never has scarcity — within a couple years it stops meaning anything and becomes operationally unmanageable (e.g., "priority table always" and "direct line to Izzy" can't scale to an ever-growing, mostly-inactive list). Rolling re-qualification keeps every tier — especially Legend — meaningful, without needing a full annual reset of the whole program.

From Walk-In to Valued Guest

Currently walk-in only with no waitlist. A digital waitlist solves three problems at once: guest experience, Chomps enrollment, and operational data.

The Enrollment Moment

A guest waiting for a table has their phone in hand, nothing to do, and high motivation — the highest-converting Chomps enrollment window you'll ever have. Guest scans door QR → joins waitlist with phone number → immediately offered Chomps signup → 500 Chomps on today's visit if they sign up now.

  • Digital Waitlist (Phase 2): Guest scans QR at entrance → enters phone → joins waitlist → gets SMS when table is ready. Frees guests to browse merch or sit outside instead of standing in line.

Schema

Core Data Model

PostgreSQL via Prisma. Every table feeds both the loyalty system and the forecasting engine.

members {
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY
  phone VARCHAR UNIQUE
  email VARCHAR UNIQUE
  name VARCHAR
  birthdate DATE
  chomps_lifetime INTEGER DEFAULT 0
  active_tier ENUM(friend, fan, obsessed, legend)
  referral_code VARCHAR UNIQUE
  referred_by UUID REFERENCES members
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ
  last_activity TIMESTAMPTZ
}

transactions {
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY
  member_id UUID REFERENCES members
  type ENUM(earn, milestone, adjust)
  source ENUM(purchase_fb, purchase_merch, social_follow,
      social_tag, social_tiktok, referral, signup,
      survey, birthday, online_store, manual, milestone,
      whoriddle)
  chomps INTEGER
  reference_id VARCHAR
  metadata JSONB
  created_at TIMESTAMPTZ
}

visits {
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY
  member_id UUID REFERENCES members
  toast_order_id VARCHAR
  date DATE
  daypart ENUM(lunch, dinner)
  fb_revenue DECIMAL(10,2)
  merch_revenue DECIMAL(10,2)
  covers INTEGER
  chomps_earned INTEGER
  multiplier DECIMAL(4,2) DEFAULT 1.0
}

social_connections {
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY
  member_id UUID REFERENCES members
  platform ENUM(instagram, tiktok, yelp, google)
  handle VARCHAR
  platform_id VARCHAR
  access_token TEXT ENCRYPTED
  follows_domo BOOLEAN
  verified_at TIMESTAMPTZ
}

waitlist {
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY
  phone VARCHAR
  member_id UUID REFERENCES members NULLABLE
  party_size INTEGER
  position INTEGER
  status ENUM(waiting, seated, left, no_show)
  joined_at TIMESTAMPTZ
  seated_at TIMESTAMPTZ
  wait_minutes INTEGER
}

Forecasting bridge: the visits table links every Chomps member to real revenue, daypart, and covers. The waitlist table captures actual wait times and party sizes. Together they transform anonymous Toast data into attributed guest intelligence.

Protection

Legal & Anti-Fraud

Chomps points have no monetary value. GFC BP LLC is protected against system failures, fraud, and program changes.

  • No Cash Value. Chomps cannot be exchanged for cash under any circumstances.
  • Program Modification. GFC BP LLC may modify, suspend, or terminate the program at any time without prior notice.
  • Non-Transferable. Chomps are not the member's property and are non-transferable/non-inheritable. Lifetime Chomps do not expire; an account may be marked dormant after 12 months inactivity per the Active Tier rules.
  • No Liability for Loss. Not liable for loss due to technical failure, system error, unauthorized access, or program termination.
  • Fraud & Abuse. Chomps obtained fraudulently will be forfeited and the account terminated.
  • Legend Tier. Crossing the threshold does not guarantee membership — invitations are curated, GFC BP LLC's sole discretion.

Every Earn Channel Must Be Automatic and Hard to Game

Because Chomps only unlocks status and never decrements, a bad actor who games their way to a high tier holds something permanent and hard to revoke without an ugly public rollback. Fraud prevention has to be tighter than a normal spend-down points program.

  • One Real Identity Per Member — phone number is the unique account anchor. Duplicate-account/multi-accounting checks run at signup and on suspicious earn patterns.
  • Verified-Event Earning Only — in-cafe purchase (Toast-tied, highest trust), social follow (OAuth), social tag (webhook-confirmed), referral (device fingerprint checked), WhoRiddle (inherits their anti-bot integrity, capped as lower-trust until proven).
  • System-Enforced Caps — high-risk channels (story tags, reviews) have hard caps enforced in code, not just written into the ToS.
  • Fraud-Review Gate Before Top-Tier Crossing — Domo Friend/Fan/Obsessed promote automatically; Legend consideration triggers a review step given the effectively-permanent nature of top-tier status.

Key UX Moments to Design Carefully

  • The Chomps Earn Moment — every earn event (in-cafe, social, referral) needs a satisfying, consistent micro-animation/toast: "You earned 347 Chomps tonight! 🍡" This is the emotional core of the whole program — treat it like a game's XP-gain moment, not a receipt line.
  • Milestone Unlock Celebration — crossing 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 / 50,000 needs a full-screen, shareable moment (confetti, Domo-kun animation, "share to Instagram" prompt) — these are the highest-value organic marketing moments in the app.
  • Tier Progress Clarity — member should always be able to see (a) lifetime Chomps, (b) trailing-12mo Chomps driving their active tier, (c) how far to the next tier — without confusing the two numbers. This is the hardest UX problem in the whole app given the dual-number model in Section 01.
  • Waitlist Enrollment Moment — the highest-converting sign-up window (Section 05). Needs to be near-zero-friction: phone number → instant 500 Chomps offer → done in under 15 seconds.
  • Legend Consideration State — members who cross 50,000 need a distinct "under review" UI state, not silence and not a fake instant promotion — manage expectation without cheapening the curation.
