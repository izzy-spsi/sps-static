Every point of Fill Rate we add, Uber earns more too

Flywheel is paid 2.5%–4% of rider payment as a 3P referral fee. Uber takes 15%–25% of the same trip. Because Driver Earnings are our GMV, every completed trip we convert pays both parties — and Uber's dollar take is 4–8x larger than ours on the identical ride. Our investment in driver conversion is disproportionately an investment in Uber's revenue.

On a $17 average Uber 3P fare, Uber nets ~$3.40 and Flywheel nets ~$0.55 per completed trip — Uber earns ~6x more than we do on every ride we fill. We only get paid when the trip completes — so we're economically aligned to push Fill Rate, and Uber's revenue rides on our success.
Live proof point (Las Vegas / Kaptyn): Fill Rate has ranged 3%–16% across live Uber 3P markets on identical driver-offer volume. The gap is conversion, not demand — exactly what Flywheel investment closes.
