01
Executive Summary
What This Process Governs

Every piece of Domo Cafe merchandise — house or licensed — moves through the same 3-phase development process before it can be sold: Concept → Pre-Production → Production. This PRD formalizes that process as a repeatable playbook, derived directly from Izzy + Amy's working session on July 26, 2026.

The core purpose of this process is twofold: (1) make sure every product idea is fully vetted and licensor-approved before real money is spent on samples or tooling, and (2) create a consistent, auditable trail that ties every SKU back to a Flow Haven (NHK/Striker) approval record.

⚠️ NHK Compliance

Any merchandise using Domo-kun IP must pass Flow Haven (Striker Entertainment / NHK) approval at two separate checkpoints — the concept mock image (Phase 1C) and the physical sample (Phase 2B) — before final production (Phase 3) begins. Skipping either gate risks breaching the NHK Merchandising License Agreement.

💡
Phase 1 — Concept
Idea → manufacturer sourced → mock image submitted to Flow Haven for concept approval.
🧪
Phase 2 — Pre-Production
Physical sample built, documented, and submitted to Flow Haven for sign-off.
🚀
Phase 3 — Production
Timeline locked, final product + samples mailed to licensor, priced, loaded into POS, promoted.

This PRD relates to, but is distinct from, the broader Domo Cafe Merchandise Portal PRD (v1.0, June 2026), which covers the software system for catalog/inventory/vendor/royalty management. This document defines the process that system will eventually need to enforce.

02
Problem Statement
Why We Need a Formal Playbook

Today, the merch development process lives entirely in Izzy and Amy's heads and in scattered conversations. There is no written reference for what phase a product is in, what's required to move to the next phase, or who owns each step.

No Shared Reference
New team members or vendors have no single doc explaining "how a Domo product gets made" from idea to shelf.
Approval Gate Risk
Without an explicit gate, a product could accidentally move to physical sampling or production before Flow Haven signs off.
Inconsistent Extras/Sample Ordering
Licensor sample-unit quantities (the "+20 extra" rule) and mailing destination (Striker vs. NHK Japan) aren't written down anywhere and rely on memory.
SKU/Barcode Ambiguity
No documented policy on when an item needs a real SKU/barcode vs. a simple internal Toast item number.

Roles
Who Does What
👑 Izzy
Final concept consensus. Approves/coordinates Flow Haven submissions. Owns production timeline, freight, and licensor mailing logistics. Final say on pricing.
🎨 Amy
Sources manufacturer + MOQ. Coordinates mock image and physical sample creation. Prepares Flow Haven submission packages. Handles pricing/POS entry.
🏭 Manufacturer (External)
Produces mock images, physical samples (with video/photo documentation), and final production run.
📋 Flow Haven (Striker / NHK)

05
Phase 1
Concept
1
Land on an approved concept before spending on samples or tooling
Owner: Team (consensus) → Amy (sourcing) → Amy/Izzy (submission)
  • A
    Conceptualize the product. Team reaches consensus on the product idea before moving forward. No solo decisions — must be a shared "yes."
  • B
    Find a manufacturer. Amy sources a manufacturer capable of producing the concept and confirms the MOQ (minimum order quantity).
  • C
    Create a mock digital image and submit to Flow Haven. A mock/rendered image representing the final look of the product is built and submitted to Flow Haven (Striker/NHK) for concept-level approval.
⚠️ Hard Gate

Do not proceed to Phase 2 until Flow Haven approval is received on the concept image. If rejected, diagnose which applies: (a) conflicts with official Domo licensing guidelines, (b) concept already exists elsewhere, or (c) concept is not on-brand — then revise and resubmit.

06
Phase 2
Pre-Production
2
Turn an approved concept into a physical, licensor-approved sample
Owner: Manufacturer (build) → Amy/Izzy (submission)
  • A
    Create a physical sample. Once the concept is approved, the manufacturer produces a physical sample. Video and image documentation of the physical sample is captured.
  • B
    Submit sample documentation to Flow Haven. The physical sample's videos/images are submitted to Flow Haven for pre-production sign-off — the final checkpoint before committing to a full production run.
⚠️ Hard Gate

Do not proceed to Phase 3 until Flow Haven signs off on the physical sample. This is the last opportunity to catch quality, licensing, or brand issues before committing to bulk production.

07
Phase 3
Production
3
Finalize timeline, licensing compliance, pricing/POS, and go-to-market
Owner: Izzy (timeline, licensing, mailing) → Amy (pricing, POS) → Marketing (promotion)
  • A
    Confirm production timeline. Confirm readiness to move to production. Lock in freight cost and timeline. Determine the target shelf date.
  • B
    Submit final product to Flow Haven + mail licensor sample units. Submit the final product — with licensing mark and final packaging — to Flow Haven as the definitive sellable format. Mail physical sample units to Striker/NHK per the licensing agreement. Order 20 extra units per variant beyond the production run for licensor stock (e.g., a 1,000-unit run split 500/500 becomes 520/520).
  • C
    Price, and load into POS. Price out the item. Assign a Toast item number (not a formal SKU/barcode) for now — see Section 10 for the SKU policy.
  • D
    Promote. Get an influencer to promote the item on social media, or at minimum produce a promotional photo/post.

09
Functional Requirements
What the Process (and Future System) Must Enforce

P0 No item may move from Phase 1 to Phase 2 without a recorded Flow Haven approval on the concept image
P0 No item may move from Phase 2 to Phase 3 without a recorded Flow Haven approval on the physical sample
P0 Every production run must include +20 extra units per variant earmarked for licensor sample stock
P0 Every rejected concept must be tagged with a rejection reason: IP conflict, duplicate concept, or off-brand

P1 Track and confirm the licensor mailing destination per item (Striker-retained vs. forwarded to NHK Japan)
P1 Track Toast item number assigned per SKU at Phase 3C
P1 Flag items for future formal SKU/barcode assignment once drop-ship/wholesale is greenlit

Open Questions
Not Yet Resolved
1. Licensor mailing destination
Do licensor sample units get mailed directly to NHK in Japan, or retained by Striker Entertainment on NHK's behalf per the executed MLA? Izzy indicated he "already knows" the answer but it was not stated explicitly on the source recording — needs written confirmation for this playbook.
2. SKU/barcode formalization trigger
Confirmed: deferred until Domo Cafe expands into drop-ship/wholesale. Toast item number is sufficient for now — proof of concept validated as working. No further action needed until expansion decision is made.
3. Rejection resolution ownership
When Flow Haven rejects a concept or sample, who owns revising and resubmitting — Amy, Izzy, or the manufacturer? Not explicitly assigned in the source discussion.
4. Approval SLA
No turnaround time was specified for Flow Haven's review of either the concept image or the physical sample. The broader Merch Portal PRD assumes a 7-business-day target — should this playbook adopt the same SLA?
