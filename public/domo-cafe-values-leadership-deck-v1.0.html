DOMO CAFE
Domo Cafe's Core Values — Part 1

Guest First · One Team · Excellence

✨
Guest First
Every decision starts and ends with the guest. Not "the guest is always right" — but staying thoughtful about the experience and owning what we can control: what they see, what they feel, and how we recover when something goes wrong.
🤝
One Team
No stars — only a team. No position matters more than the cafe's success. No cliques, gossip, public criticism, or division. When one area struggles, we help — we don't stand back and judge.
⭐
Excellence
Good enough is never the goal. Not perfection — caring about details and constantly improving: food, drinks, cleanliness, presentation, speed, accuracy, hospitality, teamwork.

DOMO CAFE
Domo Cafe's Core Values — Part 2

Energy · Integrity · Grow Every Day

😃
Energy
Our energy affects everyone around us. A calm, engaged leader steadies a shift; a frustrated one disrupts it. Energy isn't pretending everything's perfect — it's composure, purpose, and professionalism.
🤠
Integrity
Do the right thing even when no one is watching. Honesty, accountability, reliability, and the courage to speak up. Leaders apply standards fairly — never expecting more from others than they demonstrate themselves.
🌿
Grow Every Day
There is always something to learn, improve, or teach — including managers and owners. Our expectation is not perfection. Our expectation is ownership: listen, improve, take responsibility.

Our Leadership Principle
High standards without fear.
Accountability without humiliation.
Accountability is necessary — people need to know when expectations aren't met. But it should never become personal, disrespectful, or designed to embarrass someone. Correct the behavior without attacking the person.
A leader can be firm without being cruel. A leader can be direct without being disrespectful.

The Purpose of Coaching

Coach for Success — Not to Build a Case

Managers should never approach an employee as though collecting evidence to justify a future termination. That mindset makes employees feel watched instead of supported — and turns every mistake into "proof" they don't belong.

DOMO CAFE
Coaching During a Shift

Real-Time Coaching Is Brief, Specific, and Calm

Coaching should happen as close to the moment as reasonably possible — but it stays operational, not a lecture or a disciplinary conversation.
Let's greet this table before completing the side work.
Please call the order back so we know it was heard correctly.
Slow down for a moment and verify the table number.
One coach. One priority. One handoff.
During a busy shift, an employee shouldn't get five corrections from five managers at once. One leader owns the coaching, gives one clear priority, and hands off cleanly if another leader steps in — so guidance stays consistent, not confusing or embarrassing.

DOMO CAFE
When Coaching Should Be Private

Discipline in Private. Compliment in Public.

Move the conversation to a private setting when: the matter is sensitive · the employee is becoming emotional · it's a repeated pattern · it will take more than a few moments · it could embarrass the employee · it may lead to formal action.
This is a coaching conversation. I want to make sure we understand what happened and give you the support and clarity you need to be successful.
That opening line reduces ambiguity and helps prevent the employee from feeling they've already been judged.

DOMO CAFE
Should a Witness Be Present?

Normal Coaching Needs One Leader — Not a Panel

Automatically bringing two managers into every conversation can make routine coaching feel like an interrogation. A second leader is appropriate for formal discipline — not everyday development.
One Leader Is Enough
✓ Normal developmental coaching
✓ Real-time floor corrections
✓ Routine check-ins on progress
Bring a Second Leader
✓ Formal discipline / written warning
✓ Termination or suspension possible
✓ Harassment, discrimination, threats, safety
✓ Employee disputes prior conversations
The second leader isn't there to overpower the employee — their role is to observe, support fairness, and confirm what was communicated.

DOMO CAFE
How We Know Coaching Is Working

"I Understand" Is Only the Beginning

Real behavioral change shows up over time. Look for: performing the behavior correctly across multiple shifts · improving without constant reminders · holding up when it's busy · accepting feedback without defensiveness · applying it to similar situations · helping others follow the standard.
For your next five shifts, the expectation is that every arriving party is acknowledged promptly, even when you're assisting another guest. We'll check in after those shifts to discuss your progress.
That's more useful than "you need to improve your attitude." One setback doesn't automatically mean the coaching failed.

DOMO CAFE
When the Same Behavior Continues

Not Every Situation Should Be Managed the Same Way

Can't Yet
The employee needs training, repetition, or support. Response: keep teaching and practicing.
Struggling
Understands the expectation but is having difficulty applying it consistently. Response: coach, follow up, be patient.
Won't
Understands, has the ability and opportunity, and repeatedly chooses not to meet the standard. Response: move toward formal corrective action.

Recognition & Leader Accountability

Praise in Public. Accountability Applies to Leaders Too.

You stayed calm during that rush and kept communicating with the kitchen. That helped the entire team.
I noticed you stepped in to help the host without being asked. That is what One Team looks like.
You handled that guest concern with patience and ownership. Thank you.
A leadership title never excuses:
Losing composure · publicly embarrassing an employee · gossiping · conflicting instructions · playing favorites · ignoring good performance · applying rules inconsistently · failing to train someone and then blaming them for not knowing.
