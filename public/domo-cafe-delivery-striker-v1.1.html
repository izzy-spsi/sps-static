Why Delivery, Why Now

Solving for Seasonality and the Line

Domo Cafe is a walk-in-only, line-dependent model — which means our revenue is exposed to two things we can't control: seasonality (back-to-school softening sales as summer ends) and weather (a rainy Saturday can meaningfully hurt a business built on people willing to wait outside).

Delivery gives us a demand channel that doesn't depend on foot traffic, wait times, or weather — a structural fix, not just an add-on.

Delivery isn't just an order channel — it's a discovery channel. 38% of consumers use delivery apps to find restaurants they've never tried, and once discovered, 82% go on to visit that restaurant's own website. For a destination concept like Domo Cafe, delivery apps put us in front of guests who might never otherwise drive past our door.
