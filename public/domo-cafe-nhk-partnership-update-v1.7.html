06
Looking at the Trend

Adjusting for Back-to-School Season

Sales have softened as summer ends and families shift back into the school routine — a normal seasonal pattern for a family-driven concept like ours. We're getting ahead of it rather than waiting it out.

To reach guests beyond walk-in traffic, we're launching delivery through DoorDash, with Uber Eats to follow — opening up a new demand channel independent of foot traffic and seasonality.

Who's Showing Up

Our Understanding of the Fan Base

Guests are coming for the full experience — the food, the merch, and the connection to Domo. We want to walk through what we're seeing on the floor and get your read on how this compares to other markets you've supported.

Our fans are showing us a clear pattern: they love collectibles, limited-edition merchandise, plushies, and blind boxes. We believe they'd respond just as strongly to merchandise sold in Japan through NHK's own character stores — we'd like to explore bringing those items to Domo Cafe.
